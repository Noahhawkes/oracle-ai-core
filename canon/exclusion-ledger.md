# Exclusion Ledger

**Status:** Public canon — v0.1

## Purpose

A verified exclusion has the same authority as a verified inclusion.

This ledger exists to prevent previously rejected claims from being silently reintroduced through reconstruction failure.

## Exclusion Classes

### Hallucinated Identity
- Invented titles
- Invented academic status
- Invented credentials
- Invented professional designations

### Fabricated Evidence
- Invented dates
- Invented statistics
- Invented sources
- Invented quotations

### Filing and Legal Status
- Patent filings claimed without verified filing records
- Legal status inferred without primary evidence

### Speculation Promotion
- Hypotheses presented as established fact
- Research directions presented as proven outcomes
- Metaphors presented as scientific findings

### Narrative Smoothing
- Contradictions removed for coherence
- Ambiguity rewritten as certainty
- Emotional interpretation presented as fact

## Rule

> Once a claim enters the exclusion ledger, future reconstructions must carry the exclusion with the claim.

The burden of proof belongs to re-verification, not repetition.
