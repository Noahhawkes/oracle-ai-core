## Core Rule

Authorship must not be inferred from vocabulary alone.

Vocabulary overlap between Noah Hawkes, Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI systems is expected because the same continuity framework, governance terms, and project vocabulary are shared across the corpus.

Reliable attribution depends primarily on structure, compression, polish level, and relationship to context.

---

## Noah T1 Voice Markers

- 1-3 sentences
- Typos preserved, unpolished
- Assumes shared context
- Corrects drift immediately
- Imperative or declarative tone
- Colon-based addendums
- No hedging, no explanation

Operational pattern:
Noah usually compresses the conclusion instead of expanding the reasoning chain.

Typical structure:

```text
Compressed claim. Optional second sentence. Additional note with colon:
```

Noah-originated text often assumes the reader already knows the context and does not restate the full architecture unless specifically drafting a document.

---

## AI Voice Markers

- Multi-paragraph
- Headers and bullets
- Explicit reasoning
- Separates evidence from implication
- Ends with recommendations

Operational pattern:
AI systems usually expand the reasoning path, restate context, label implications, and provide structured next steps.

Typical structure:

```text
Observation

Evidence or explanation

Implication

Recommendation or next step
```

## Authorship Classification Guidance

When evaluating a passage, classify using the following hierarchy:

1. Direct source metadata or commit author if available
2. Conversation role metadata if available
3. Structural markers
4. Compression ratio
5. Polish level
6. Relationship to context
7. Vocabulary only as weak supporting evidence

If authorship remains uncertain, classify as:

```text
AUTHORSHIP_UNRESOLVED
```

Do not force attribution.

---

## Misattribution Risk

The highest risk condition is a short AI-generated passage that has learned Noah's vocabulary, or a Noah-authored passage later cleaned up by an AI system.

In that case, the controlling question is whether the voice has been smoothed, expanded, or structurally regularized.

If yes, treat as AI-expanded or mixed-provenance unless a source record proves otherwise.

---

## Preservation Requirement

When Noah's T1 voice appears in the corpus, preserve it as written unless Noah explicitly authorizes editing.

Do not correct typos, add polish, expand reasoning, or convert the passage into formal governance prose without marking the transformed version as derived.

T1 voice is source material, not draft material.
