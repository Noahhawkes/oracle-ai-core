## Critical Epistemic Structure

The engine preserves truth by separating all intake into three tiers:

### Observed Fact

Information directly confirmed by client intake or source material.

### Operational Assumption

A logical inference drawn from available intake that requires verification before becoming part of the official record.

### Structural Gap

A missing policy, conflicting record, undefined risk, unclear workflow, or unresolved dependency that must be addressed to complete the Core.
