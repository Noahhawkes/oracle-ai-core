## 4. How Do We Deliver It?

Delivery follows the Build the Core protocol.

### Step 1: Observe

Capture the business as it actually operates.

Input sources may include:

- Policies
- Emails
- PDFs
- Screenshots
- Voice notes
- Meeting transcripts
- SOPs
- Proposals
- Employee handbooks
- CRM exports
- Service workflows
- Pricing rules
- Customer scripts
- Software instructions
- Leadership preferences
- AI usage examples

### Step 2: Copy

Classify and preserve the intake without narrative smoothing.

Every record is sorted into epistemic categories:

#### Observed Fact

Information directly supported by the intake.

#### Operational Assumption

A logical inference requiring client verification.

#### Structural Gap

A missing policy, conflicting record, undefined ownership point, unclear workflow, or unresolved risk.

### Step 3: Store

Compile the final deliverable into a protected continuity record.

The record may be stored through:

- Client delivery folder
- PDF package
- Word documents
- Training deck
- Protected hard drive
- Versioned repository templates
- Quarterly audit log

The final product must be inspectable, updateable, and governed.
