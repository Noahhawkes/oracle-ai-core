## 1. Why this archive exists

Noah's central problem is not how to make a chatbot sound human. It is how to preserve the evidence and context of a human life so that time, software boundaries, memory loss, context-window limits, repeated summaries, and unreliable AI claims do not erase the reasons behind decisions. The work asks what it would take to preserve reasoning, relationships, revisions, commitments, emotional meaning, creative work, and the original records from which those interpretations are drawn.

This has an intensely human motivation. Noah describes the loss of his father while he was young as an important origin of his concern with the disappearance of lived reasoning. Objects, photographs, and other people's memories can survive while the person's ability to answer new questions does not. This personal motivation is not empirical proof of any theory. It explains why the problem matters to its creator.

The program refuses to equate archival fidelity with mind transfer, or an AI's convincing imitation with a person's survival. Those questions remain open. A faithful archive must preserve both the ambition and the uncertainty.

## 6. SOV1, authority and the tribunal

SOV1 is best treated as a governance/sovereignty layer over capabilities, not an independently empowered actor. The fundamental question is not merely whether a model can execute an action, but who authorized it, under what scope, with what reversible controls and receipts.

The tribunal-like architecture aims to preserve criticism, reversals, uncertainty, competing interpretations, and correction history. It is designed to prevent the familiar promotion chain:

`AI speculation → repeated summary → apparent consensus → assumed fact → canon`

The governing distinctions are:
- **Token origin:** where words entered the system.
- **Actual authorship:** who created the words.
- **Transport:** who copied or moved them.
- **Approval:** who accepted a proposal.
- **Evidence:** what independently supports a claim.
- **Canon:** what the recognized authority has promoted.

Candidate is not canon. Historical state is not current state. Repetition is not independent verification. The witness must not become the author.
